Our Branches of Care

Each service is a pathway to growth, carefully designed to support your child's unique developmental journey

1:1 In-Home ABA Therapy

Our dedicated Board Certified Behavior Analysts (BCBAs) and Registered Behavior Technicians (RBTs) develop and implement completely individualized therapy plans in the setting where your child is most comfortable—your home. This naturalistic approach helps children generalize skills more effectively for lasting, meaningful progress.

Parent Coaching and Family Support

We view parents as essential partners in their child's success. Our coaching services are designed to provide you with a deep understanding of behavioral principles and arm you with effective, evidence-based strategies to manage challenging behaviors, teach new skills, and support your child every day.

Community and Professional Partnerships

A child's success is a team effort. We bridge the gap between therapy, school, and other services by collaborating with educators, Speech-Language Pathologists (SLPs), and Occupational Therapists (OTs). We offer expert staff training and ongoing consultative support to create a consistent and holistic support system for your child across all environments.

Who We Are

Navigating a new diagnosis or the daily challenges of raising a neurodivergent child? At Branch and Beacon, we are here to serve as your family's guiding light and source of hope. We specialize in early intervention (ages 0-5), providing high-quality care that blends the science of Applied Behavior Analysis (ABA) with the joy of play. This dedicated, one-on-one therapy is provided in the home, your child's natural environment where they are most comfortable.

Committed to moving our field away from generic, high-intensity treatment models, our therapy is always centered on your child's unique interests and needs. We are dedicated to equipping parents with the tools to be successful, while our child-centered approach lets kids be kids. By embedding therapeutic goals into natural play and daily routines, we focus on teaching communication, building social skills, and replacing challenging behaviors with functional skills. We are honored to walk alongside your family as we discover strengths, encourage independence, and create a path to success together.

BCBA-owned and operated

We are personally and professionally dedicated to making a lasting, positive impact on your family.

To us, you are never just a number; we are partners on your journey, and we are honored to walk it with you.

Neurodiversity-Affirming Care

We honor and respect your child for who they are.

Our care plans are designed to celebrate their unique interests and ways of learning. We empower children by prioritizing self-advocacy, teaching functional communication that works for them, and always honoring their assent (their "yes" and "no").

Joyful & Effective Learning

We believe therapy should be fun.

Our play-based and child-led approach embeds learning so seamlessly into your child's natural play that they are simply enjoying themselves, intrinsically motivated to learn and grow.

A Truly Unified Team

Your child's success depends on consistency.

We build a circle of support by collaborating closely with everyone involved in their care—from family and caregivers to teachers and other professionals—to create a seamless path to success across all environments.

Our Guiding Lights

Expert BCBAs who serve as beacons of knowledge and compassion on your child's journey

AA

Annie Angel

Clinical Director, BCBA

Welcome– I’m so glad you’re here! For more than 12 years, I've had the privilege of working in the field of ABA. I earned my Master's degree in Applied Behavior Analysis from The Chicago School of Professional Psychology and have spent the last 7 years as a Board Certified Behavior Analyst (BCBA) working across home, school, clinic, and community settings. My passion is early intervention, and my practice is rooted in a compassionate, whole-child approach. I believe that true progress happens when a child feels safe, understood, and happy. That's why I intentionally incorporate play, sensory integration, and emotional regulation into my practice. As a Level 4 PFA-SBT certified clinician, I focus on understanding what makes your child light up and use those unique interests to build critical new skills in a way that is respectful, fun, and empowering.
